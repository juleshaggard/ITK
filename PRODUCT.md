# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

B2B technology leaders and founders evaluating how to position, launch, or reposition their companies. They need to quickly understand the firm's expertise, process, credibility, and fit before deciding whether to book a call.

## Product Purpose

Present In The Kitchen as a sharp, experienced positioning partner for post-product-market-fit B2B technology companies. The page should create confidence, explain the offer clearly, demonstrate credibility, and convert qualified visitors into introductory calls.

## Brand Personality

Bold, editorial, confident.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages with interchangeable card grids and stock visual language.
- Timid typography or neutralized styling that weakens the existing identity.
- Decorative complexity that competes with the positioning story.
- Unnecessary departures from the supplied site's established visual language.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the recognizable identity and use the supplied site as the primary visual authority.
2. Let typography carry the hierarchy through deliberate contrast, scale, and pacing.
3. Communicate expert confidence through direct copy and decisive composition.
4. Keep the path from understanding the offer to booking a call obvious.
5. Prefer distinctive, purposeful choices over generic marketing conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard operability, visible focus states, sufficient text contrast, reduced-motion support, semantic interaction states, and responsive legibility.
