How much does it cost?

+

The minimum commitment to work with In The Kitchen is $20,000 and about five weeks. Our creative studio delivers just like top 1% agencies, for about half the price.

We’re early stage. Are we a fit?

+

Our positioning process is built for post-product-market-fit, scaling, or at-scale companies. If you’re still trying to figure out what the market wants from you, it’s probably too early.

What’s different about growth-stage and at-scale positioning?

+

Quite a bit. The early days are about finding your niche. Scaling is about expanding your market, which often entails selling multiple products, marketing to different segments, and speaking to different personas. In The Kitchen specializes in digging into the harder questions that come with growth. 

Can we start next week?

+

Unlikely. We are fortunate to be busy. Lead times range from weeks to months to quarters, so please don’t be shy if you’re interested. If you want our help, we really want you to have it. 

Are you full-service?

+

No. In The Kitchen is all-in on positioning. We only do creative work for our positioning clients. And we only do brand, web, and sales decks (because those are the deliverables where positioning comes alive.) We are very happy to work with your internal team, contractor, or agency to make sure they are set up to run with our work.
